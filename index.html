Questions we get.

How is this different from a lead vendor?

We don't sell leads. We deliver booked appointments with borrowers who've confirmed a business-purpose deal, a property with price and ARV, a close date, and their experience tier — tracked through to funded loans. Lead vendors sell contact records, often to three to five buyers at once.

What exactly counts as a pre-qualified appointment?

It's written into the agreement, not left to interpretation: a booked appointment with a borrower who has self-reported a business-purpose, non-owner-occupied deal, a property with purchase price and rehab budget or ARV and a close date, a deal size above your minimum, deals completed in the last 24 months, and cash available to close. All self-reported. We screen — you underwrite.

What if you send us borrowers who don't qualify?

You set the bar at onboarding: minimum experience tier, minimum liquidity, loan floor, states. We screen every booking against it. If your underwriting declines someone, that still counts as delivered — underwriting is your decision and your business. Want the bar raised? We'll raise it, and volume drops proportionally. We tell you that in advance rather than argue about it later.

How fast do we see appointments?

Usually week two, from your existing database. Paid search goes live around day 14 to 21 and takes about a month to settle. The guarantee window is 90 days for exactly that reason.

Who owns the ad account and the data?

You do, both. The ad account is created in your name with your billing. Your contacts are yours — export on demand, and complete handover of every contact, conversation, and appointment record if we ever part ways. That's in the contract before anyone asks.

Can this create compliance problems for us?

It's built the other direction. Campaigns run only in states where you're licensed, listed as a contract exhibit and mirrored in every platform's geo settings. Investment-property language on every asset. No prohibited claims, no purchased or scraped lists. Any proof-of-funds letter is issued by you, under your name.
