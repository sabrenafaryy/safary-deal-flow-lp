Questions we get.

How is this different from a lead vendor?

They sell a contact record, often the same one they sold three other lenders. We generate the borrower, qualify him, and book him — with the property, price, ARV and close date already captured.

What happens to my pipeline if I stop?

Nothing changes. Every lead lands in your own system as it arrives, the ad accounts are in your name from day one, and full export is in the contract. You already have your data.

How fast will I see funded loans?

Appointments in weeks 1–4, mostly from reactivating your list. First fundings from cold traffic in month 2–3 — closing takes 30–60 days even for a ready borrower. Month six beats month three.

What if an appointment isn't qualified?

The definition is agreed in writing before you sign, and every appointment shows in your report with its outcome. If it doesn't meet the definition, it doesn't count toward the guarantee.

Brokers or only lenders who fund?

Both, and hybrids. The guarantee is on appointments — which don't care whether you fund or place. The break-even math differs; we run yours on the call.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly, from an account in your name. Media never runs through my account and it's never marked up.

Do you work with my competitors?

Not in your market, for your product. Territory is agreed before signing.

How do you handle compliance?

Business purpose only, geo-fenced to your licensed markets, NMLS where required, consent on every form, registered messaging throughout. No invented reviews or results, ever.
